Our Process

From Discovery to Settlement — And Beyond

Domenic guides every client through a structured, transparent journey designed for long-term success.

01

Discovery Call

We start with a conversation to understand your goals, timeline, and financial position.

02

Financial Assessment

A thorough review of your income, assets, liabilities, and credit profile.

03

Strategy Design

We design a personalised lending strategy aligned with your short and long-term objectives.

04

Lender Comparison

We source and compare competitive options across 30+ lenders to find the right fit.

05

Presentation

We present your options clearly — explaining rates, fees, features, and trade-offs.

06

Application & Approval

We manage the entire application and liaise with lenders on your behalf.

07

Settlement

Seamless coordination with all parties for a smooth, stress-free settlement.

08

Ongoing Support

Regular reviews to ensure your lending structure continues to work for you.
